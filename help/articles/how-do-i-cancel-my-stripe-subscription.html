How do I cancel my Stripe subscription?

Paid Harpax subscriptions are billed through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from your Cambric AI billing portal — cancellation is self-serve, takes a minute, and there's no retention call required.

Demo phase

Harpax is currently in demo. If you're using the free demo download, there is no subscription to cancel — just delete the app. Paid subscriptions and the billing portal will roll out alongside the full release.

Cancel in three steps

  1. Go to the Cambric billing portal at cambric.ai/billing and sign in with the email you used to subscribe.
  2. Click Manage subscription. This opens the Stripe-hosted Customer Portal.
  3. Click Cancel plan and confirm.

You'll receive a confirmation email from Stripe within a minute. Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period — you won't lose access immediately.

What happens after you cancel

  • Your subscription stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Stripe doesn't pro-rate cancellations — the time you've already paid for is yours.
  • You won't be charged again. Stripe will not run another renewal on a cancelled subscription.
  • Your local Harpax install keeps working. Harpax runs on your machine. Cancelling the subscription doesn't disable the app, delete your session history, or revoke your local rules. Anything subscription-only (if any tier ever gates features behind it) will simply stop on the next billing date.
  • You can re-subscribe later at any time using the same billing portal.

Refunds

Cambric AI does not automatically refund unused time on a cancelled subscription. If you have a special circumstance — a duplicate charge, an accidental renewal, an issue with the service — email contact@cambric.ai and we'll look at your case directly.

Updating your payment method instead

If your goal is to change cards rather than cancel, use the same billing portal: cambric.ai/billing → Manage subscription → Update payment method. No cancellation needed.

Deleting your account vs. cancelling your subscription

These are different actions:

  • Cancel subscription — stops billing, keeps your billing-portal account active so you can re-subscribe later.
  • Delete account — removes your billing-portal record entirely. Email contact@cambric.ai to request account deletion under GDPR or for any other reason.

Note: Harpax itself runs locally and doesn't require either of the above. See How do I create an account? for the distinction between the local app and any optional Cambric account.

Trouble cancelling?

If you can't access the billing portal, don't remember the email you subscribed with, or hit any other snag — email contact@cambric.ai from the address tied to your subscription and we'll handle the cancellation manually.
